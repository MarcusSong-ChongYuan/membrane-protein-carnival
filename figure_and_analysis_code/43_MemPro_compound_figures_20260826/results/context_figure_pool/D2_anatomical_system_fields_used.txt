Analysis unit for heatmap: unique canonical protein × anatomical system, derived after deduplicating protein × disease × system.
Each displayed cell is a 2×2 Fisher exact test against the same mapped disease-associated protein universe. Colour = Haldane–Anscombe-corrected log₂ odds ratio; black dot = BH-FDR < 0.05 with observed support ≥20.
Anatomical systems are formal Uberon-derived multi-label mappings and may have hierarchical overlap; they are not mutually exclusive and totals do not sum to 100%.
Bar chart shows raw unique canonical disease-associated proteins by anatomical system; labels show unique diseases.
